Rules & explanations
  • Move: a step blocked by another player is skipped. Its point is spent; later steps continue from your actual position.
  • Attack: reach 2 grids straight or 1 diagonally and push 1 grid. A wall, ridge, or edge blocking the push causes 1 HP damage. Another player blocks without damage.
  • Dodge: choose an adjacent escape direction. The next attack triggers one sidestep. A blocked destination makes it fail; landing on a bomb still triggers it. Dodges expire each round and do not stack.
  • Scan Here: 1 point records the bomb count in the eight grids around you. 3×3 Scan costs 3 points and records nine numbered clues. A number does not prove its own grid safe.
  • 3-Grid Scan: 2 points reveals bombs directly in three grids, with no numbers. Other grids keep their visibility. Found initial bombs stay visible until removed; planted bombs follow the visibility setting.
  • Plant Bomb: 1 point on an adjacent non-terrain grid. Break Wall: a separate 2-point action targeting an adjacent wall. Both use straight directions only.
  • Clues are dated snapshots. Planting, disarming, and explosions do not update them. Rescan to refresh; clues expire with the selected visibility mode.
  • Disarm: spend 1 point to check one adjacent grid, including diagonals, and safely remove its bomb. No flags are needed.
  • From round 21, bombs cause instant death. Blocked-push damage stays at 1 HP.
  • The last survivor wins. If no bombs remain, the living player with the highest HP wins; tied leaders share victory. This is checked after each action.
